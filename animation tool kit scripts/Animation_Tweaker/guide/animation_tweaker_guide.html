Animation Tweaker guide

Click any button, field or brush in the window below to see what it does and how to use it. Or pick a workflow at the bottom and step through it; the window shows you where to click.

    1. Select the character mesh and click Use selected.
    2. Go to the frame you want to fix and pick a keying Mode.
    3. Click Sculpt. A blue copy of the mesh replaces it in the viewport.
    4. Shape the copy with a brush from the Sculpt brushes shelf.
    5. Click Commit. The fix is stored as a tweak and keyed.
